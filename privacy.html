Privacy Policy

Financebro AI · Effective date: July 6, 2026 · Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Yusuf Demirtas, trading as Financebro Studio ("Financebro", "we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Financebro AI mobile application (the "App"). Financebro AI is a financial education and coaching service. It is not a brokerage, not a licensed financial advisor, and does not provide financial advice.

For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 (KVKK), and Brazil's General Data Protection Law No. 13.709/2018 (LGPD), the data controller (controlador, under the LGPD) is Yusuf Demirtas, an individual trading as Financebro Studio. Financebro Studio is a trading name, not a separate company; the person responsible for your data is named here so you always know who that is. You can reach us at financebrostudio@gmail.com.

1. AI-generated content

Coaching responses in the App are generated by artificial intelligence (Anthropic's Claude). They may be incomplete, out of date, or incorrect, and must never be your sole basis for any financial decision. Always do your own research and consider consulting a licensed professional. The App shows no live prices and no market data — the coach has no access to either.

  • To provide the App and personalize coaching — performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To process your subscription — contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To send the daily brief and other notifications — your consent, which you can withdraw anytime in Settings (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • To understand product usage and fix crashes — our legitimate interest in improving and securing the App (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable (Art. 6(1)(c)).

For users in Brazil, the LGPD (Art. 7) provides equivalent legal bases for these activities, including performance of a contract, consent, legitimate interests, and compliance with a legal obligation.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for cross-app advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. International data transfers

Your account and content are stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Some processors (analytics, crash reporting, AI) operate in the United States, so your data may be transferred there. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses; for transfers of data out of Brazil, we rely on the international-transfer bases permitted under the LGPD (Art. 33). By using the App you understand your data may be processed outside your country.

6. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is removed promptly from our live systems; residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days on a rolling schedule. Aggregated, non-identifying analytics may be retained.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, KVKK, Brazil's LGPD (Lei nº 13.709/2018), and the CCPA (as applicable), you have the right to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. You can exercise the two most common rights directly in the App:

  • Delete your account & data — Profile → Settings → Delete account (GDPR Art. 17 / KVKK).
  • Export your data — Profile → Settings → Export my data (GDPR Art. 20).

For any other request, email financebrostudio@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority (in Türkiye, the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu; in the EU, your national supervisory authority; in Brazil, the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD)).

8. Analytics & identifiers

We use first-party analytics (PostHog) and crash reporting (Sentry) that rely on device and usage identifiers to measure how the App is used and to fix problems. These are used for our own product purposes only — not for advertising. We do not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS), encrypted storage of your authentication session in the device Keychain, and row-level access controls so each user can only access their own data.

10. Children

The App is intended for users aged 16 and older and is not directed to children or to anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. When we may disclose your information

We may disclose information where we believe in good faith that it is necessary to comply with a valid legal obligation — a court order, subpoena, or lawful request from a public authority — or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or to protect the safety of a person. We will not disclose your information for any other reason. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

12. If our business changes hands

Financebro Studio is currently operated by an individual. If it is incorporated as a company, reorganised, merged, or its assets are transferred to another business, your information may transfer with it so that the App can keep running and your account is not interrupted. Anyone receiving your information stays bound by this Policy until we tell you otherwise, and we will notify you in the App if the person or company responsible for your data changes.

13. Do Not Track

We do not track you across other apps or websites, so a "Do Not Track" signal has nothing to switch off. The App contains no advertising SDKs, requests no advertising identifier, and shares nothing for advertising purposes. Our behaviour is the same whether or not such a signal is sent.
