We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is removed promptly from our live systems; residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days on a rolling schedule. Aggregated, non-identifying analytics may be retained.

One limit worth stating plainly: events already sent to a third party before you deleted your account or switched a setting off — to Meta for advertising measurement, or to our analytics and crash-reporting providers — are held under that provider's own retention rules and we cannot recall them. Deleting your account stops anything further being sent.

8. Analytics & identifiers

We use first-party analytics (PostHog) and crash reporting (Sentry) that rely on device and usage identifiers to measure how the App is used and to fix problems. Those two are used for our own product purposes only — never for advertising.

Separately, from version 2.6, Meta's measurement software sends install, app-open, and purchase events so we can tell whether our advertisements worked, while Advertising measurement is switched on in Settings. It creates its own identifier for this App on your device. It is not given Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA): we do not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt, and we switch off the identifier collection in Meta's software as well, so there are two independent reasons it never leaves your device. Without that identifier, Meta cannot join what it sees here to what you do in other companies' apps.

Note that Apple's App Store privacy label for this App nonetheless declares tracking. Apple counts embedding advertising software of this kind as tracking even when it is not used for that purpose, so the label is stricter than our actual behaviour rather than a description of it.

13. Do Not Track

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so a "Do Not Track" signal has nothing to switch off, and our behaviour is the same whether or not one is sent. A "Do Not Track" signal is in any case a browser signal, and the App is not a browser — there is no way for it to receive one.

From version 2.6 the App contains one advertising-related component: Meta's measurement software, described in sections 4 and 8. It does not request Apple's advertising identifier. The switch that actually controls it is in the App itself — Profile → Settings → Advertising measurement.

"Shine the Light" (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.83). We do not share personal information with third parties for those third parties' own direct-marketing purposes, and never have.

Two things limit it: we apply Meta's Limited Data Use setting, which restricts how Meta may use data from users in California; and you can stop it entirely at Profile → Settings → Advertising measurement.
