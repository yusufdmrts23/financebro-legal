Frequently asked

Is this financial advice?

No. Financebro AI provides financial education and coaching only. It is not licensed advice and nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Responses are AI-generated and can be wrong — always do your own research and consider a licensed professional.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iOS Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Financebro AI → Cancel. Your access continues until the end of the current period. Refunds are handled by Apple.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently erases your account, profile, and conversations.

How do I export my data?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Export my data for a JSON copy.
