User Data Deletion

Financebro AI · Last updated: November 20, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Financebro AI account and the personal data associated with it. Deletion is built into the app and deletes the records listed in section 3 from our live database (copies in encrypted backups expire as the backups roll over, normally within 30 days). Some of our providers keep their own copies; section 3 explains what they keep and how to ask for it to be deleted. You can also email us instead of using the app.

The data controller is Yusuf Demirtas, an individual trading as Financebro Studio. You can reach us at financebrostudio@gmail.com. This page supplements our Privacy Policy.

1. Delete your data from inside the app

This is the fastest route and works for every account, including accounts that were never signed in to.

  1. Open Financebro AI.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm when asked. The deletion runs immediately and you are returned to the start of the app.

2. Delete your data by email

If you can no longer access the app — for example you have already removed it from your device — email financebrostudio@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”.

So that we delete the right account and no one else's, please send the request from the email address linked to your Apple sign-in, or tell us the approximate date you first used the app together with any display name you set. We may verify the request against the account details we already hold, and we use what you send us for verification only for that purpose. We handle deletion requests as the law that applies to you requires.

3. What is deleted

Deleting your account is a permanent deletion, not a deactivation. Your account record is deleted, and the records linked to it in our database are deleted with it:

Your profile — name, age, country, currency, occupation, monthly amount you can invest, experience, risk tolerance, goals, time horizon, investor archetype, language, time zoneYes

Deleting your account in the app does not delete what three of our providers keep under your account identifier: our subscription provider, RevenueCat (your purchase history, the App's tracking-permission status and, if an Apple Ads campaign led to your install, that campaign), our analytics provider, PostHog (your analytics profile and events), and our crash-reporting provider, Sentry (crash reports, which Sentry deletes automatically at the end of the retention period set for our account, currently up to 90 days). You can ask for these to be deleted in your deletion email, or by emailing us after deleting in the app; we normally have them deleted within 30 days of your request, except for anything the law requires us to keep. What our AI provider, Anthropic, keeps is held under its own retention policy (Privacy Policy, section 4). Copies inside our hosting provider's encrypted backups expire as the backups roll over, normally within 30 days. Our hosting provider's operational, security and sign-in audit logs, which can contain your account identifier and IP address, are not deleted when you delete your account; they are kept under the hosting provider's and our account's retention settings. We may keep aggregated, non-identifying statistics that cannot be linked back to you or to any individual.

4. What deletion does not do

It does not cancel a paid subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us, and only Apple can cancel them. If you subscribe, cancel separately at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions — or on your device: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Deleting your account while a subscription is still active will not stop future charges.

Deleting your account also does not remove the app from your device. Delete the app itself in the usual way if you no longer want it installed.

It does not recall advertising-measurement events already sent to Meta; those are held under Meta's own rules. (What our analytics and crash-reporting providers hold is covered in section 3.) Deleting your account from inside the app switches Advertising measurement off on that device. Once the app has been fully closed (swiped away in the app switcher, or ended by iOS) and started again, the app sends nothing more to Meta from that device. Until then, including when you return to the app from the background, Meta's software, which is already running, may still send Meta the events described in the Privacy Policy, section 4, What stops, and when, such as an app open or a subscription bought in that time, with your IP address and device information, and fetch its own settings. An install report that iOS itself may already have scheduled under Apple's SKAdNetwork is not affected (Privacy Policy, section 4). If you delete your account by email, or the app is still installed on another device, switch it off there (Profile → Settings → Advertising measurement) or remove the app. The Privacy Policy (section 4) explains what is sent to Meta.

5. Deletion is permanent

We do not restore deleted accounts, and your lesson progress, streak, review deck and coach history are not recovered. If you install the app again afterwards, you start in the app as a new user (the Privacy Policy, section 6, says what our providers may still hold).

If you want a copy of your data before deleting it, use Profile → Settings → Export my data first.

6. Your rights

The deletion route above, together with an email request for the copies our providers keep (section 3), is how we honour the right to erasure under the EU/UK GDPR (Article 17), Türkiye's KVKK, Brazil's LGPD, and the CCPA/CPRA in California. You may also ask for access to your data, its correction or portability, or restriction of its processing, object to its processing, and withdraw consent. See the Privacy Policy for the full description of those rights and how to exercise them.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you may complain to your local data protection authority — in the EU your national supervisory authority, in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office, in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK), and in Brazil the ANPD.
