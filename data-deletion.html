User Data Deletion

Financebro AI · Last updated: September 1, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Financebro AI account and all of the personal data associated with it. Deletion is built directly into the app, takes effect immediately, and does not require you to contact us — but you can email us instead if you prefer.

The data controller is Yusuf Demirtas, an individual trading as Financebro Studio. You can reach us at financebrostudio@gmail.com. This page supplements our Privacy Policy.

1. Delete your data from inside the app

This is the fastest route and works for every account, including accounts that were never signed in to.

  1. Open Financebro AI.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm when asked. The deletion runs immediately and you are returned to the start of the app.

2. Delete your data by email

If you can no longer access the app — for example you have already removed it from your device — email financebrostudio@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”.

So that we delete the right account and no one else's, please send the request from the email address linked to your Apple sign-in, or tell us the approximate date you first used the app together with any display name you set. We will verify the request against the account details we already hold, and we will not use anything you send us for verification for any other purpose. We respond within 30 days, and normally much sooner.

3. What is deleted

Deleting your account is a hard delete, not a deactivation. Your authentication record is destroyed and every row keyed to it is removed with it:

DataDeleted
Your profile — name, age, country, currency, occupation, goals, investor archetype, languageYes
Your conversations with the AI coach, and every message in themYes
Lesson progress, quiz results and your review deckYes
XP, streaks, daily goals and badgesYes
Your subscription record held by us, and usage countersYes
Push notification tokens and notification logsYes
Any coach responses you reported to us for moderationYes

Nothing of yours survives in our live systems. Residual copies inside encrypted backups are purged within 30 days on a rolling schedule. We may keep aggregated, non-identifying statistics that cannot be linked back to you or to any individual.

4. What deletion does not do

It does not cancel a paid subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us, and only Apple can cancel them. If you subscribe, cancel separately at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions — or on your device: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Deleting your account while a subscription is still active will not stop future charges.

Deleting your account also does not remove the app from your device. Delete the app itself in the usual way if you no longer want it installed.

5. Deletion is permanent

We cannot restore a deleted account. Your lesson progress, streak, review deck and coach history are gone for good. If you install the app again afterwards you will start as a completely new user.

If you want a copy of your data before deleting it, use Profile → Settings → Export my data first.

6. Your rights

The deletion route above is how we honour the right to erasure under the EU/UK GDPR (Article 17), Türkiye's KVKK, Brazil's LGPD, and the CCPA/CPRA in California. You may also ask us to access, correct, port, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. See the Privacy Policy for the full description of those rights and how to exercise them.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you may complain to your local data protection authority — in the EU your national supervisory authority, in Türkiye the KVKK (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu), and in Brazil the ANPD.
